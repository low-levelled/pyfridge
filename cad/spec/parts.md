## Facts that change the CAD model

- **Handles are bolt-on bar handles** (AED73593202/212/251 door handle assemblies, MJB63190001 mounting studs, AEJ73440302 brackets, MBL65278201 end caps). Not pocket handles. Vertical bars on both fridge doors, horizontal bar on the freezer drawer (AED73593102 / AED37133172). Matches the 2.5" depth delta with/without handles.
- **Door-in-Door = "Home Bar"** in LG naming: case MBN63442501, foam door ADD74296801, gasket MDS64610508, handle MEB62915403, covers MCK67480101 / MCK67979601 / MCK67979701.
- **Ice maker lives in the left door** (Slim SpacePlus): ice bank lower hinge AEH74156201, ice bin door gasket MDS64239202, ice bucket AKC73249303, ice maker kit AEQ73110210/212, auger motor EAU61083522, assembly EAU60783850.
- Capacity 26.6 cu ft (17.7 fridge / 8.9 freezer). 4 spill-safe split cantilever glass shelves, 9 door bins, 2 humidity crispers, 1 deli/"Fresh Room" tray.
- Compressor TCA38151706 — LG linear inverter.

## Door design (SM spec table)

- Door design: **Side Rounded** [C]
- Handle type: **Bar** — "Contoured Doors with Matching Commercial Handles", SmartPull freezer handle [C]. Bar diameter [P].
- Door finish: PCM stainless [C]; foam-filled doors, cyclopentane PU foam [C]
- Hinges: hidden top hinges under hinge covers; top hinge held by a rotating lever (CCW left door, CW right), door lifts off middle hinge pin; lower/middle hinge has height-adjust bolt (CCW raise, CW lower); auto-closing when open < 30° [C]
- Left door carries: dispenser, in-door ice maker, foldable heated mullion (2.3–2.9 Ω heater), water tube + 2 harnesses through top hinge [C]
- Right door carries: Door-in-Door ("Home Bar") with button release on the handle, removable inner case, ColdSaver panel [C]
- Per-door width: nothing published. Derived from LG figures: door-open depth 47⅝ − depth w/o door 29 = 18⅝ in (door width + hinge offset); width at 90° 39¼ − 35¾ = 3½ in ⇒ each door swings 1¾ in past its cabinet side (= LG "door edge clearance w/o handle 1¾"). ⇒ each door ≈ **17.5–17.75 in (445–451 mm), 50/50 split** [C derived]. Modeled 50/50.
- Handle mounting: bar on two stud fasteners (¼ in Allen), locked by set screws (3/32 in fridge, ⅛ in freezer) — standoff bar, no visible screws [C, sibling OM]
- Dispenser recess W×H and vertical position [P] — modeled 241 × 356 mm centered 55% up the door. Control panel (membrane, white LED) sits to the **left** of the recess [C, spec-sheet photo].
- Freezer drawer front height [P] — modeled 530 mm; spec-sheet photo proportions suggest 20–21 in of the 68⅜ in case, consistent.
- Hinge mechanics [C]: left door middle/lower hinge has height-adjust nut; right door is set by snap rings on the middle hinge pin; middle pin carries a plastic sleeve; hinge cover on top screwed to case. Swing angle and pin offset not published [P].
